Implement the cross-bureau and officer case assignment/transfer workflow with mandatory justification capture, assignment history tracking, and interactive reassignment modal.

Purpose: Allow authorized supervisors and officers to delegate cases across AICTE cells while maintaining full institutional accountability and unbroken transfer lineage.
Output: Assignment service, assignment API endpoint, CaseReassignmentModal component, and dossier integration.
</objective>
