<task type="auto">
  <name>Build AI intelligence service and router endpoints</name>
  <files>
    backend/app/services/ai_intelligence.py,
    backend/app/api/v1/ai.py,
    backend/app/api/v1/router.py
  </files>
  <action>
    Create backend/app/services/ai_intelligence.py:
    - Text preprocessor: Tokenization, lowercasing, stopword removal, and n-gram extraction.
    - TF-IDF Vectorizer & Cosine Similarity calculator: Computes normalized term-frequency inverse document frequency vectors across complaint subjects and descriptions, returning ranked similarity scores and intersecting keywords.
    - Smart Triage Rule & Heuristic Engine:
      * Maps domain keywords to AICTE bureaus (e.g. "ragging", "hostel", "warden" -> BUR-RAGG; "fee", "refund", "surcharge", "capitation" -> BUR-APPR; "curriculum", "syllabus", "internship" -> BUR-POL; "affiliation", "court", "disciplinary" -> BUR-LEGAL).
      * Factors in institution history: checks prior approved grievances from the same institution to boost confidence.
      * Produces transparent rationale breakdowns (e.g., "Found 4 high-affinity keywords for BUR-RAGG: ['ragging', 'warden', 'hostel', 'intimidation']", "Institution 1-100234564 has 3 previous cases routed to Anti-Ragging Bureau").
    - Triage Apply Service:
      * Requires permission (ADMIN, SUPERVISOR, or authorized OFFICER).
      * Updates ComplaintCase bureau_id and/or category inside a database transaction.
      * Creates CaseAuditEvent with action="AI_RECOMMENDATION_ACCEPTED" and details of the suggestion applied.
    
    Create backend/app/api/v1/ai.py:
    - POST /api/v1/ai/duplicates/check
    - GET /api/v1/ai/duplicates/{case_ref}
    - POST /api/v1/ai/triage/suggest
    - POST /api/v1/ai/triage/apply
    Mount in backend/app/api/v1/router.py under prefix="/ai", tags=["Explainable AI Intelligence"].
  </action>
